About Luke

An Otago farrier who turns up, does the job well and gives you a straight answer.

Luke Ure is a farrier based in Otago, New Zealand, working with horse owners across the region.

His approach is simple: do the job well, turn up when he says he will, and give owners honest advice about their horse's feet. Whether it is a pony club mount, a competition horse or a hack that gets ridden on the weekends, every horse gets the same care and attention.

Being a mobile farrier means Luke spends his days on the road, visiting properties, stables and farms around Otago. He can set up a regular cycle for your horses so they never go too long between visits, and he is happy to work alongside your vet on remedial cases.

The approach

What you can expect

Horse first

Quiet, patient handling. A relaxed horse is a safer horse, and a better job gets done.

Reliable

Luke turns up when he says he will and lets you know if something changes.

Honest

Clear advice about what your horse needs, and nothing it doesn't.
